# Circle TD sprite atlas — provenance

`atlas.png` / `atlas.json` are generated by [`scripts/gen-circle-td-sprites.cjs`](../../../../scripts/gen-circle-td-sprites.cjs).
Regenerate with `npm i -D @napi-rs/canvas` then `node scripts/gen-circle-td-sprites.cjs`.

## Unit sprites (original work)

`tower-fast`, `tower-air`, `tower-slow`, `tower-splash`, `tower-damage`,
`creep-normal`, `creep-fast`, `creep-air`, `creep-hard` — original
neon-geometric vector art drawn procedurally by the generator script. No
third-party source; created for this project to match the site's design system.

## FX frames (derived from Kenney Particle Pack, CC0)

`fx-glow`, `fx-star`, `fx-muzzle`, `fx-smoke` — downscaled composites of four
textures from the **Kenney Particle Pack** (https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack).
Kenney assets are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication):
free for any use, commercial included, no attribution required. Credited here as
a courtesy.

Only these derived, downscaled frames ship in the atlas. The full raw Kenney
kits are **not** committed (see `.gitignore`: `public/games/circle-td/kenney_*/`);
the generator reads them locally at regeneration time only.
