Arcfire brainstorm mockup (design reference, not normative)

Deck+: layout A with C's best parts

One layout. It keeps A's labeled command deck, precise ± controls, and big FIRE, and adds C's always-visible weapon carousel, drag-to-aim, and floating HUD. Both tanks are the default Vanguard; players can still swap to Scout or Wedge before a match.

Preview: Deck+ battle screen (desktop, or a phone in landscape)

What came from where

1 · Weapon carousel (from C): all 10 drafted weapons always visible; used ones are dimmed and struck through.
2 · Weapon info (new): what the selected weapon does. It replaces C's floating tag, which matters more with 30+ weapons to learn.
3 · Labeled ± controls (from A): precise angle and power nudges.
4 · Drag-to-aim (from C): drag from your tank; direction = angle, length = power. Either input updates the other.
5 · Floating score + turn pills (from C): the battlefield stays clean.
6 · Big FIRE (from A).

The deck takes ~30% of the height, the same as A. On the smallest phones the carousel scrolls horizontally.
