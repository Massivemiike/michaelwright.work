Arcfire brainstorm mockup (design reference, not normative)

The draft screen, and where it sits in the match flow

Picks alternate until each side holds 10 weapons from today's pool of 24. Tap any card to inspect it: the panel explains what it does and shows a live looping preview, rendered by the real engine since the sim is deterministic and cheap.

1 MODE→2 TANK + SETTINGS→3 DRAFT→4 BATTLE→5 RESULTS
Preview: draft screen (desktop, or a phone in landscape)

How it reads

Pool grid: each card shows its glyph, name, and behavior tag (BLAST, SPLIT, BOUNCE…). Taken cards dim and wear their owner's color dot.
Trays: the AI's picks sit across the top in blue and yours along the bottom in red, 10 slots each.
Detail panel: description, three quick stat bars, the live preview, and the PICK button.
On phones: the detail panel becomes a bottom sheet and the grid scrolls.
