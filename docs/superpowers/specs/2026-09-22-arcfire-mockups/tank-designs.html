Arcfire brainstorm mockup (design reference, not normative)

Which belt-driven tank design?

All three are proper tracked vehicles: a continuous belt looping around a toothed drive sprocket, road wheels, and an idler. The treads and wheels are animated the way they'll roll when a tank uses a move. Left: detail view. Right: actual in-game size on a phone (red vs blue), where tanks tilt to hug the slope.

A · Vanguard

A classic main battle tank: a long, low track with four road wheels, sprocket and idler, return rollers, a sloped hull, a wide turret, and a long gun with a muzzle brake.

+ Unmistakably a tank.
− The small wheels get busy at phone size.

B · Scout

Compact and chunky: a tall rounded track with three big wheels, a friendly dome turret, and an antenna. Closest to Pocket Tanks' arcade charm.

+ Most readable at small sizes; the big wheels make the belt motion obvious.
− More arcade than serious military.

C · Wedge

Angular sci-fi, still tracked: a faceted hull, a hex turret, a twin-rail gun, and a six-sided track with glowing links.

+ The most on-brand with the site's neon-geometric style.
− Less classic; the angled track reads slightly less like a belt.

My pick

B · Scout, with C's glowing track links. On a phone a tank is only ~45px wide, and three big wheels on a tall track keep the belt motion unmistakable at that size, while C's glowing links keep it neon.
